## Design

The seventh slide promotes the real `/parts` store with an illustrated exploded rackmount server, four directly clickable component categories (CPU, ECC RAM, SAS/SSD, RAID), a chassis link, the primary store CTA and buying advice. Localized generation shortcuts point to the existing `/servers/hp/gen8` through `gen12` pages. The outer frame, inner image border and server cover are removed. A single chassis silhouette and four independently cropped clickable pieces reuse the original WebP; each named component, label and hit area travel together, on desktop and phones. The slide-level spherical halo has no hard border: radial alpha stops feather its rim into the page background while it continues beneath the controls and proof rail to the bottom of the desktop viewport. Mobile retains the shared stable carousel height and a circular glow with transparent extremes. Product claims are limited to the existing catalogue's categories, specifications, technical part numbers, stated compatibility and comparison. The art is a conceptual illustration, not a specific SKU or evidence of stock, pricing or manufacturer certification.

After the image is decoded, CPU, RAM, SAS/SSD and RAID lift sequentially from the chassis at 320/780/1240/1700 ms offsets, each rising for 920 ms. Its label reveals 520 ms into that lift. All have emerged by 2620 ms and then hover gently by 5–7px at distinct speeds; artwork stays in one source URL. A return to the slide replays the extraction. Deliberate Pause freezes the bob without preventing the initial reveal, focused/hovered targets stay easy to select, off-screen/hidden animations pause, and reduced-motion users see static pieces instead. An artwork error shows the catalogue links instead of leaving a pending animation.

The first six slides and their order remain; seven slides rotate every 6000 ms, or six when Studio is unconfigured. The original first-fold globe, GPU, fade animation, existing service directory, localized links, statistics and pause controls are retained. Seven navigation selectors fit a single row on desktops and two rows on phones. Ordinary pointer interactions pause temporarily and automatically restart the six-second interval exactly 2000 ms after the last click or pointer release, including repeated selections, Studio examples, region choices and globe drags. A resting pointer and click-induced focus do not leave the carousel paused. The deliberate Pause control, reduced-motion default, keyboard exploration, unclicked hover, hidden-document and off-screen guards remain; Play also dismisses a temporary pause immediately. The single 104,112-byte WebP (1200×800) is assigned to the five visual layers only when the parts slide is selected. No additional photo, package, third-party asset, API or database work.

Image brief used the built-in image generator: premium photorealistic exploded-view concept of an open unbranded 2U rackmount server with recognizable Xeon-like processor, ECC DIMMs, hot-swap enterprise SAS/SSD tray and RAID PCIe controller hovering above its chassis; deep navy studio, brushed metal, cyan rim light; no text, logos, watermarks, or UI. Optimized asset: `website/public/assets/img/home-parts-exploded.webp`.
