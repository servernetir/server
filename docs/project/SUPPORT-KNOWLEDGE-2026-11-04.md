## Operational boundaries

- Hermes drafts are not proof that a check, payment or configuration change happened.
- Working email with deliberately external authoritative DNS does not justify an
  unsolicited nameserver/IP change merely to clear a panel warning.
- Existing SQL access is not server-wide database administration or proof of a
  particular .NET runtime. A read-only IIS pool view is not successful activation.
- n8n resource/price/management promises require the current offer and workload needs.
- An expired payment session does not prove customer lateness or transfer failure.
  Do not request another payment while the original transfer is being reconciled.
- Payment receipt, billing status, wallet credit and paid service delivery are
  separate facts. Legacy manual credits must be excluded from replay.
- Refund timing uses the new three-business-day instruction; no completed payout is
  claimed without its actual evidence, and the time origin is not repeatedly reset.
- Provider costs and approved profit/handling charges must be funded before company
  balance is spent. A deposit does not mean an IP change was performed.

The UI still reports the separate Hermes API as needing configuration. The evidence
here establishes approved native knowledge and working native retrieval, not
successful consumption by an external VM/Bale assistant or newly granted execution.
