## Changes in this release

- `VpsTcCatalog` no longer accepts literal Array payment fees, even with a reviewed money contract and a zero-fee historical invoice. Both current credit-payment fees must be numeric zero. The current calculated quote, invoice binding, tax, currency, gross ceiling and USD/TRY normalization safeguards remain.
- `VpsTcApi::serviceResources()` adds the fixed authenticated GET. The legacy `vmUsage()` callable returns unsupported without authentication/network traffic; console also states that no verified reseller grant exists. No missing snapshot/task/password route is invented.
- Preflight adds **billing-only** mode through the existing protected admin action and CLI. It reads balance, payment methods and fees (three authenticated GETs; login/recovery only as needed), skips catalog/forms/quote calls and refuses to claim native currency repair through the historical invoice fallback. Supported currencies, unique fee IDs, numeric fields and the selected zero-fee Credit Balance method are checked. Reports include only a fixed safe account-read failure category.
- Admin displays the portal renewal checklist, expiry policy, scoped reference-product traffic/entitlements and outstanding quote contract. Existing admin role, CSRF and throttle middleware remain in place.
- Reviewed catalog terms can describe `throttled` monthly combined traffic with `overuse_mbps`. `CloudPlan::trafficLabel()` renders volume/direction/throttle/absence of metered overage fees in fa/en/tr. Malformed terms do not fall through to a fabricated hard-cap label.
- A revised commercial mapping can resolve traffic while retaining the two pending billing questions. Its exact account/product/configuration scope hash and pending list must match; the old approval cannot be reused after editing a mapping. No mapping, margin, sales switch, price snapshot or customer record is changed by installing source.
