## Financial and access validation

Markup is 500 basis points (5%) on known provider cost, with the owner's declared FX acquisition overhead at zero and applicable usage VAT separate. Settlement chooses the greater of catalogue consumption cost and provider-reported estimated cost, preserves admission-time FX/rate/tax snapshots and rounds upward. The percentage applies before whole-Toman rounding; comparing two separately rounded integer amounts does not measure the exact markup.
